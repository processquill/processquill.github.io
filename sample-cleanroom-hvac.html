Qualification Report — Cleanroom & HVAC System CR-201

A condensed public preview of a fictional cleanroom/HVAC qualification report for a Grade C support / controlled background area.

HVACHEPA integrityClassification
Portfolio note: This is a fictional ProcessQuill demonstration sample prepared to show document structure and technical depth. It does not contain client, employer or proprietary source material and is not intended for operational use.

Executive qualification summary

Objective. Demonstrate that the HVAC-supported cleanroom suite provides the required environmental control through verified airflow, pressure cascade, temperature/RH control, HEPA integrity and particulate classification performance.

Qualification decision. The demonstration report records acceptable HEPA filter integrity, airflow velocity/volume, pressure differential cascade, temperature, relative humidity, at-rest non-viable particulate classification and recovery time. A minor balancing adjustment is documented and closed during qualification.

Scope represented in the sample

  • Air handling unit serving the cleanroom suite
  • HEPA terminal filters
  • Room pressure cascade
  • Airflow distribution
  • Environmental setpoints
  • At-rest cleanroom classification
  • Recovery capability

Example acceptance topics

The portfolio document includes HEPA integrity, defined positive pressure cascade, 18–22 °C temperature control, 40–60 %RH, at-rest particle classification within room limits and recovery to an in-limit state within the approved time.

Boundary management

The sample explicitly separates HVAC/cleanroom qualification from microbiological monitoring-program validation and gowning qualification, which are treated as separate controlled programmes.
