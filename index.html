ABOUT PROCESSQUILL

Technical understanding first. Documentation second.

ProcessQuill is built for regulated work where a document has to explain not only what was done, but why the evidence is sufficient for a GMP decision.

Ramesh Gupta combines biomedical engineering, scientific work, quality control, qualification & validation engineering and GMP/QA experience in Germany. The focus is the interface between process knowledge, risk, technical evidence and controlled documentation.

Support can include equipment qualification, validation planning and reporting, sterilization, aseptic processing, sterile filtration, cleanroom/HVAC systems, risk management, deviations, CAPA, change control, SOPs and technical reports.

The goal is not to make a document look complicated. The goal is to make the technical decision clear, traceable and defensible.

FAQ

Practical questions before a project starts.

Clear scope at the beginning usually makes regulated documentation faster and easier to review.

Can ProcessQuill work with an existing company template?

Yes. Existing templates, procedures, protocols and document conventions can be used as the basis for drafting or revision.

Can you review a document instead of rewriting it?

Yes. Review can focus on structure, technical logic, acceptance criteria, traceability, wording and the strength of the conclusion.

Can documentation be prepared in English or German?

Yes. Professional technical documentation can be prepared or revised in English and German.

Do you provide remote support?

Yes. The fixed-price packages are designed for remote documentation support. On-site execution, measurements, testing or larger validation programmes are scoped separately.

What information is needed to start?

Typical inputs include the process description, URS or technical data, existing procedures, available qualification or validation evidence, the company template and the expected deliverable.

Are fixed prices available for every project?

No. Fixed prices cover clearly defined documentation packages. Larger or more complex GMP projects are quoted individually after scope review.
