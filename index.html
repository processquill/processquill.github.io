ABOUT RAMESH GUPTA

A career built where engineering, quality and GMP meet.

I am a pharmaceutical qualification and validation professional with a multidisciplinary career spanning biomedical engineering, scientific work, quality control, qualification & validation engineering and GMP/QA support in Germany.

My work focuses on the point where complex technical evidence has to become a clear GMP decision. I have worked with equipment and process qualification, validation planning and reporting, sterilization, aseptic processing, sterile filtration, cleanroom/HVAC systems, risk management, deviations, CAPA, change control, SOPs and technical reports.

I combine an engineering mindset with formal GMP, quality-management and audit qualifications. That allows me to look beyond formatting: I challenge the logic of a document, connect requirements to evidence, identify gaps, define meaningful acceptance criteria and present the conclusion in a way that is practical for Production, Engineering, Validation and QA.
