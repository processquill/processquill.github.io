ABOUT THE SPECIALIST

A career built where engineering, quality and GMP meet.

Ramesh Gupta is a pharmaceutical qualification and validation professional whose career has developed across biomedical engineering, scientific work, quality control, qualification & validation engineering and GMP/QA support in Germany.

His work sits at the point where technical detail must become defensible documentation: equipment and system qualification, process validation, sterilization, aseptic processing, sterile filtration, cleanroom/HVAC qualification, risk management, deviations, CAPA, change control, SOPs and technical reports.

What distinguishes his approach is the ability to move between the engineering reality of a process and the quality-system logic expected in a controlled GMP document. The goal is not simply to produce more pages — it is to make complex evidence clear, traceable and usable for review, approval and inspection.
