ABOUT RAMESH GUPTA

A career built where engineering, quality and GMP meet.

I am a pharmaceutical qualification and validation professional with a multidisciplinary background spanning biomedical engineering, scientific work, quality control, qualification & validation engineering and GMP/QA support in Germany.

My work focuses on the point where technical evidence has to become a defensible GMP decision. That includes equipment qualification, validation planning and reporting, sterilization, aseptic processing, sterile filtration, cleanroom/HVAC systems, risk management, deviations, CAPA, change control, SOPs and technical reports.

I combine an engineering mindset with formal GMP, quality-management and audit qualifications. I do not simply format documents: I challenge the logic, connect requirements to evidence, identify gaps and make the conclusion practical for Production, Engineering, Validation and QA.

Strong GMP documentation should make the technical decision easier to understand — not hide it behind complexity.
