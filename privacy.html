ProcessQuill / Privacy

PRIVACY

Privacy information for this website.

ProcessQuill is intentionally kept technically simple. The site does not use advertising trackers, analytics scripts or a server-side contact form.

Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you access the website, technical request data such as IP address, browser information and request timestamps may be processed by the hosting provider as part of delivering and securing the service. GitHub's own privacy information applies to that processing.

No analytics or advertising cookies

ProcessQuill currently does not use Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, advertising pixels or similar visitor-tracking scripts. The website does not intentionally set its own marketing or analytics cookies.

Contact by email

The contact form on the homepage does not transmit or store form data on the website. It prepares an email in your local email application. Personal information is received by ProcessQuill only if you choose to send that email.

External image delivery

To preserve the readable quality of the current profile photograph and professional certificate previews, some images are presently delivered through an external image content-delivery network. When such an image loads, the image provider may receive technical request information needed to deliver the file. ProcessQuill does not use that image delivery for advertising or behavioural tracking.

External links

Links to email or other external destinations are activated only when you choose to open them. The privacy practices of an external destination are governed by that provider.

Contact

For privacy questions concerning ProcessQuill communications, contact processquill@gmail.com.
