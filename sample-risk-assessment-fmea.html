Quality Risk Assessment — Automated CIP Cycle FMEA

A condensed public preview of a detailed FMEA for introduction of an automated CIP cycle on a 500 L product-contact mixing vessel.

FMEAICH Q9(R1) thinkingControl strategy
Portfolio note: This is a fictional ProcessQuill demonstration sample prepared to show document structure and technical depth. It does not contain client, employer or proprietary source material and is not intended for operational use.

Executive risk summary

The demonstration assessment evaluates product-quality, contamination-control, equipment and data-integrity risks associated with a new automated clean-in-place cycle. Twelve credible failure modes are assessed across cleaning chemistry, time/temperature/flow parameters, sensor reliability, equipment design, recipe selection, operator intervention and electronic release evidence.

Before additional controls, the fictional example classifies 3 risks as High, 6 as Medium and 3 as Low. Proposed controls reduce residual risk so that no residual High risk remains. Acceptance is conditional on completion of defined qualification, calibration, recipe-control and verification actions.

System context

The sample system is a fixed 500 L stainless-steel mixing vessel with a recirculating CIP loop using pre-rinse, alkaline wash, intermediate rinse, final rinse and drain steps.

Evidence linked to the FMEA

  • Detergent / conductivity response
  • Temperature
  • Recirculation time
  • Flow / pressure
  • Final-rinse endpoint
  • Cycle completion status
  • Retained electronic batch records

Method

Severity, probability and detectability are scored on a 1–5 scale. RPN supports prioritization but is not treated as the sole acceptance criterion; severity-based escalation can apply independently. The sample is designed to show how risk controls become qualification and verification actions rather than remaining isolated spreadsheet entries.
